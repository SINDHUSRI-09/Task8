📊 Sample Superstore Profit Report - README
Overview
This dashboard provides a comprehensive visualization of the sales, profits, and quantities across various regions, categories, and sub-categories of a superstore. The purpose is to analyze performance trends and identify the most and least profitable sectors of the business.

📁 Key Sections
🔹 KPIs (Top Metrics)
Sum of Sales: 2.30M

Sum of Profit: 286.40K

Sum of Quantity Sold: 38K

These represent total metrics across all regions and categories.

🔹 Filters
Sub-Category (Left Sidebar): Includes checkboxes for filtering based on product types like Accessories, Appliances, Chairs, etc.

🔹 Region-wise Tabs
Data is segmented across six main regions:

Central

East

NorthEast

South

SouthWEST

West

📊 Visualizations
1. Bar Chart – Sales by Sub-Category & Region
Compares sub-category sales across different regions.

Highlights top-selling items like Chairs, Phones, and Tables.

2. Line Chart – Profit by Year
Tracks yearly profit growth from 2016 to 2019.

Shows a clear upward trend, ending at 93K in 2019.

3. Bar Chart – Profit by Sub-Category
Displays the profitability of each sub-category.

Top Profits: Copiers, Phones, Accessories.

Losses: Machines, Fasteners, Bookcases.

4. Pie Charts
Profit by Category: Technology (highest), followed by Office Supplies and Furniture.

Profit by Segment: Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office.

Profit by Region: West leads (37.78%), followed by South and East.

🔍 Insights
Top-Performing Category: Technology (50.9% of profits).

Most Profitable Sub-Category: Copiers (56K).

Region with Highest Profit: West (108.24K).

Profit Growth: Increased steadily from 2016 to 2019.
